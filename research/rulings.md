The compact version of `model.md` for the lead author and chapter writers: every ruling, R1 to R33, with its
confidence, lead records, required wording and what would change it. For the tables, the alternatives and the
reasoning, open `model.md` at the same R number; if the two differ, `model.md` governs. Both files are kept in
the repository as the standing authority for later edits, so both are kept level with the records and with what
the site prints (last brought level in round 4d, 3 October 2026). Where a record as it now stands is more
careful than a ruling's description of it, the record governs for what is shown or stated, and the ruling's
grade governs for what is inferred.

Confidence is high, medium or low, with "medium-high" and "low-medium" between. **[CHANGE]** marks a difference
from the theory as it stood before the rebuild (the `theory.ts` of 1 October 2026); the site has since been
rewritten to these rulings. "Records" are lead records only: open the JSON before citing. Four tags mark a
record of a weaker kind, in the forms the site prints: "(a transcript)" for Scott's 2018 and 2024 interviews,
which rest on machine transcripts and are support only; "(read from an image)" for a document or texture not yet
seen in play; "(as described)" for a scene or a sound held as a description; "(a fan wiki's account)" for a
count, a comparison or a detail taken from a fan wiki. Mark an inference once, where it enters. The site's
markers go by the kind of claim: "most likely" for a short inference; "the model reads" for the archive's own
interpretation, which the model tells; "may be", with its reason, for a possibility held loosely and not told;
"leans" where two readings are close. A chapter carries the mark of its main claim, and a beat of the storyline
the mark of the weakest thing it tells (R27 has the rules). Use descriptors, never book-only names: the Cassette
Man, the Cassette Man's daughter, the crying child, the older brother, the fifth child.

## Outside the story

`outsideStory` lists records of a kind that carries no story weight and that nothing cites. A record that a
chapter cites, lists as counter-evidence or files under `further` is never listed. When a chapter cites a joke,
a mechanic, a tale or a staged prop, it prints the kind beside the citation.

- `non-canon-per-scott`, Releases Scott ruled non-canon: FNaF4's Halloween Edition; Sister Location's Custom
  Night play, not its cutscenes.
- `declared-imaginary`, FNaF World's adventure: not its frame voice.
- `unplaced-adaptation`, Into the Pit: every record of it, secret minigames included, that the Into the Pit
  section does not cite. Replaces `stories-within`.
- `company-set-dressing`, The level as built, inside Fazbear's own products: never the frame, never an intrusion
  (the dancing figure and the rabbit's part in Pizza Party are story).
- `mechanics-and-rosters`, Mechanics and rosters: tuning values and controls; a mechanic the game explains or
  names is a story record.
- `not-evidence`, Cut, unused, data-only or unverified: each entry says what the item is, not which way it
  points.
- `development-commentary`, How the games were made: unless the statement works as a ruling (Scott's account of
  two working names is cited by the labels note).
- `promotion`, Promotion and release logistics: unless it carries in-world text; the "1979" that ends Secret of
  the Mimic's teaser is not a release date.
- `jokes-and-trolls`, Jokes, trolls and hoaxes.
- `community-and-merchandise`, Scott and the community; merchandise; films.
- `author-context`, Scott himself: biography.

## What the thesis no longer says

It no longer says that a designer "learns that something of a dead person can persist inside a machine" and
"uses that first to kill": the order is the other way round, and the man who calls the machines his own making
says he never learned how the dead came to stay. It no longer says that "what keeps the dead in machines is
called Remnant". It no longer says that fire is "the characters' repeated answer": it is one man's answer, once.
And the Mimic walking out of a fire is no longer offered as fire failing. After review: nor that Afton killed
the Cassette Man's daughter "in the company's costume", nor that the five children's bodies "go into the
mascots"; nor "Nobody designed that", "It does not end Afton" or "burns with him inside". After the third
review: nor "partner", "his partner's daughter" or "the two men behind the restaurants" (no record says partner;
the game says "old friend"); nor that the game inside the game "works where fire did not". After the fact-check:
nor "the newest building" (no record calls the Pizzaplex new, the latest or the largest; the paragraph has "the
company's Mega Pizzaplex"). UCN's lean is no longer in the paragraph: it is told in its own chapter (R33). The
paragraph now tells the nights the player plays, the five cornering their killer and the son going after his
father, and it carries one marker, "on the model's reading", on what the rabbit in the code is.
