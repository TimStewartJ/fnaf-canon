# Working papers of the October 2026 theory pass

These are the papers the pass produced on the way to the present theory, kept as they were written. They are
history: nothing here is shown on the site, and nothing here overrides the records, the chapters or
`../rulings.md`. File paths inside them point at the working session's folders, which no longer exist; the
files they name are the ones in this folder. Ids are those of the day: since then the people group `emily`
became `cassette`, the chapter id `partners` became `hen-and-wa`, and the free tag `toysnhk` became
`one-you-should-not-have-killed`. Some papers quote names the archive does not use (names from the novels, fan
labels), in order to rule on them.

In the order the work ran:
