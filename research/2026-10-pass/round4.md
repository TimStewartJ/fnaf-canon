   available on the model's reading and 1986 is where it takes the bracket to end. The ruling now says that
   this is the model's step and not a printed limit. "Before the 1987 restaurant" stays medium-high.

### 7.1 Not accepted, with the reason

- **"By the scale a low reading takes 'may be'" for the voice that refuses release (verify-4).** The premise
  was a line of R28, "whose it is, low". That "low" graded which victim the accuser is, as step 21 has it ("low
  for which victim") and as R16's own confidence line had it ("which child, low"). R16 (3) has called "he runs
  the night" the model's reading, told as one, since the second review, and the whisper under the rare lines
  was listed there as its support. One sentence added in round 4b read R28 the other way and is corrected.
  The join is the archive's own interpretation, which the model tells, so the marker is "the model reads",
  and the chapter `ucn` stands. Its figure, supplied now, is medium at its lower end, inside W3.
- **"A beat may not be firmer than its chapters" (verify-1, as a possible reading).** It is not the rule and
  was never a ruling. A beat carries the mark of the weakest thing it tells. The five beats named tell nothing
  of the low claim of the chapter they link to.
- **Tagging Pizzeria Simulator's credit of "William Afton" as a fan wiki's transcription (verify-1,
  verify-4).** Not accepted as put. Every cast list in the archive is a fan wiki's transcription, and a part's
  name quoted from one line is cited like any transcribed line of a game, with no tag. The tag goes where an
  argument is made from the shape of a list. That rule does ask for one change in the other direction: the
  comparison of two cast lists in `ffps-credits-returning-voices` should carry "(a fan wiki's account)" where
  it is argued (five sentences, listed under 4d).
- **Changing "stays as it burns" and "the building burns" (layerfix-time, layerfix-pages, verify-4).** Not
  accepted. The record has "a fire breaks out in the pizzeria" and three machines shown burning, and its own
  title is "the pizzeria burns during the speech". What may not be said as shown is that the building burns
  down, or that all four machines burn. R28 now says so.
- **Moving "Shadow Bonnie" to tier 2.** Two records' notes now quote Scott's guide, "RXQ aka Shadow Bonnie".
  The archive still has no record of that line with its own source, so the label stays in tier 3, as a marked
  alias. A record of the guide's line would move it.
- **A figure for "most likely" in the three ties that print "haunts" (layerfix-people, decision 9).** Not
  refused: the three (Elizabeth and Circus Baby, Elizabeth and Scrap Baby, the Cassette Man's daughter and the
