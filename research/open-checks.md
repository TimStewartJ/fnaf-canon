# Open checks: records to add, and things nobody could verify from here

Written at the end of the October 2026 theory pass. The archive's conclusions rest on its records, and a record
is only as good as how it was read. This file lists what the pass could not check, and the records that would
let a hedge in the theory be dropped or would test a ruling directly. R-numbers are the rulings in
`rulings.md`. When one of these is done, add or correct the record, then look at the chapter's "what would
change this".

## How the records were read

- **Game dialogue, tapes and on-screen text** come from fan-wiki transcripts. They are cited without a tag,
  like any quoted line.
- **Images** on file (170) carry captions written from the image; the last verification looked again at only
  some of them. Where a record describes a screen that is not on file, it says "(as described)", or "(read
  from an image)" when the wiki's image was looked at and not kept.
- **Scenes described from a fan wiki's summary and not watched** carry "(as described)" or "(a fan wiki's
  account)" wherever a chapter leans on them.
- **Scott's posts** were verified at their source or in an archive of it. His two video interviews (2018 and
  2024) are held as machine transcripts and were not checked by ear: "(a transcript)".
- **Every cast list** (Sister Location, Pizzeria Simulator, Help Wanted, Security Breach, Help Wanted 2) is a
  fan wiki's transcription of the credits. No credits roll was captured.
- Nothing was checked in play. Nobody in this pass ran a game.

## Checks that need the game, a capture or a recording

1. **Secret of the Mimic: the work order and the credential, seen in play.** The year 1979 ends the official
   teaser; in the game it was read from images of a work order (9/07/79) and a credential whose placement is
   unconfirmed (R2, R24). Every date for that night is marked as the teaser's.
2. **Secret of the Mimic: that the cabinet F10-N4 is the first machine's mind.** The model joins it from four
   records. A line of the game that states it would make R21 (1) "shown". Twelve records of M1 and of the voice
   speaking as Fiona carry the person tag `f10-n4` on the strength of that join.
3. **Secret of the Mimic: where on the radio the cabinet's voice first takes over.** Three records say it is not
   stated. The game's audio files or subtitles may mark it.
4. **Secret of the Mimic: the bodies in character suits.** That they are the earlier technicians is the usual
   reading and rests on the voice's account (`secret-of-the-mimic-previous-technicians`). A capture is wanted.
5. **Secret of the Mimic: Fiona's and David's deaths, and the Murrays' marriage.** No recording states either
   death in as many words, and none says "wife". A line that does would firm steps 2 and 3 of the order.
6. **Secret of the Mimic: the two yellow prototypes.** Their names come from file labels
   (`secret-of-the-mimic-rnd-proto-pizzeria`). How they look in play would let the floor of the five's bracket
   (1980) rest on something seen.
7. **RUIN: the costumes in the sealed room.** That they are Murray's is the wiki's comparison
   (`ruin-mimic-lair-mcm-mascot-suits`). A side-by-side capture would settle it.
8. **RUIN: the rabbit's shadow in the hideout** (`ruin-vanny-hideout-glitchtrap-shadow`) and **the wrecked
   Bonnie in the hidden room** (`ruin-glamrock-bonnie-remains-hidden-room`): both described from the wiki.
9. **RUIN: whether Helpi says "the entity".** Two records disagree. If he does, the lower-case word is the
   game's own (R26).
10. **Help Wanted 2's main ending, watched** (`hw2-faz-force-ending`): whether the girl is shown clearly enough
    to be Cassie, and what the bot holds out. Its place between Security Breach and RUIN is "most likely" (R22).
11. **Security Breach: Princess Quest III's ending** (`security-breach-pq3-help-wanted-hub-trapped-door`), and
    whether the Burn It All Down ending shows the buried pizzeria collapse. The second would be the best
    single support for R19 (4).
12. **Security Breach's credits roll, captured.** The cast list gives "Vanessa" and "Vanny" to two performers
    (`security-breach-credits-vanessa-vanny-two-parts`). It lowered "Vanny is Vanessa" to medium in round 4c;
    the layout on screen may differ from the transcription.
13. **Ultimate Custom Night: the second voice.** A whispering voice is reported under three rare lines
    (`ucn-happy-frog-never-rest`, `ucn-nedd-bear-forever`, `ucn-orville-he-tried-to-release`). Whether it is
    one voice across the three, and whether any other line has it, needs a listening check. "He runs the
    night" (R16 (3)) would be firmer or weaker for it.
14. **Pizzeria Simulator's last scene, watched for Lefty.** The record has three machines among the flames,
    from the wiki's captures (`ffps-completion-fire`).
15. **Help Wanted: the clip that plays near the rabbit in the hub**, by ear, against the first tape's greeting
    (`hw-glitchtrap-uses-tape-girl-voice`).
16. **FNaF2: the fifth body in Give Gifts, Give Life.** One frame, reported by the wiki
    (`fnaf2-give-gifts-give-life`). It is one of the four counts in which the yellow bear marks the one more.
17. **Special Delivery: whether the inbox showed a date beside each email.** The records say it was not
    verified. If it did, those days were on screen and R24 (2) needs a second look.
18. **Three fan videos nobody in the pass watched.** Game Theory's "Why FNAF Will Never End" (2015) was the
    source of a guess on the Box page and is now attached to nothing. Whether "The FINAL Theory", part 2,
    argued that Mike Schmidt is Michael decides that Theorists entry's source and one count on the entry for
    that video. The date of "FNAF 6, What was in the BOX?" (given as 2018-02-04) was not checked.

## Left as they are, on purpose

- **Record ids are stable handles.** No id was renamed or deleted in the pass, so a few say more than their
  records now do (`security-breach-rooftop-vanessa-alive`, `secret-of-the-mimic-log-m1-as-fiona`,
  `hw-glitchtrap-uses-tape-girl-voice`) or carry a fan label (`…-toysnhk`, `fnaf2-shadow-freddy`,
  `into-the-pit-prologue-mci`). Record pages print them labelled "Record id"; they are not part of the claim.
- **People tags were not swept as a whole.** The rule (a tag stays only where the game at that spot names the
  person or shows the figure) was applied to the records the fields pass named and the ones it passed through.
- **Teaser records.** Twenty-seven records of a teaser picture with printed words are graded "shown" and
  eleven "stated"; either can be defended, and they were not made uniform.
- **"The newest game".** The storyline's first beat calls Secret of the Mimic the newest game, which is true of
  the archive's list of releases today. It needs changing when a later game is added to `games.json`.
- **Safari was not tested.** WebKit could not be started on the machine that built the site; the interface
  checks ran in Chromium.

## Decisions that are the owner's

- **"Cassidy".** Scott used the name in 2020, in posts about a licensed product and two rejected film scripts
  (three records). The archive still does not use it for any child, because no game prints it.
- **The Survival Logbook** stays out, as decided on 1 October 2026.
- **Mike Schmidt.** Three chapters say that the strongest evidence usually cited for "Mike Schmidt is Michael"
  is in a book the archive does not admit, without saying what the book says (R13, R27). If that sentence is
  unwelcome, it can go from `michael`, `open` and `fnaf1-restaurant` with no other change.
- **The Theorists entry "The Cassette Man is Henry"** is kept, with text that says the name comes from the
  novels and is not used here. It is the one place a book-only name is printed.
