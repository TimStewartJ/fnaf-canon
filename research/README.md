# Research notes (not shown on the site)

The public site presents only the current model. Its history, and the notes of earlier reviews, live here.

- `CHANGELOG.md`: notable changes to the model's conclusions, newest first. Each entry says what changed, why, and
  what was deliberately left alone. The entry for 2026-10-02 covers the pass that produced the present theory: six
  audits of the evidence base, an adjudicator's rulings (R1 to R33), three adversarial reviews, the rewrite into
  35 chapters, and then ten fact-check reports, one revision pass by every writer, three rounds that corrected the
  rulings' wording (one grade moved, in the last), two passes by a records editor, a final pass on two readers' memos
  and an independent verification. Ruling numbers quoted anywhere in this folder refer to that pass.
- `audit-ledger.ts`: the adversarial theory review of September 2026, kept as history. It lists 35 findings, each
  with its problem, its resolution, the evidence involved and the theory chapters that now argue the point. The
  findings are worded as they were written in September, so they describe the model as it stood then; where the
  October 2026 pass overtook, refined or kept a finding, the last sentence of its resolution says so.
  `scripts/validate.ts` checks that every record id and chapter id in the ledger still exists.

What is not here: the working notes of the October 2026 pass (the analysts' memos, the full rulings with their
arguments, the review reports and the fact-check reports) were kept outside the repository. Their conclusions are in the theory itself:
each chapter states its confidence, lists the records that push back, and says what would change it.
