## 2026-09-26: the plush's pupils
- Checked by pixel inspection of the game sprites.
- There are two sprites: tracking white pupils, and empty black eyes.
- By scene: pupils appear in the house sequences; empty eyes in the Night 3 outdoor sightings (flower head, yard, girl's-room doorway); no plush inside the diner.
- The Night 6 'I will put you back together' is shown over two white dots only.
- Conclusion: it is not two characters (the same voice speaks throughout). The pupils mark when the plush is watching, which supports the 'real watching toy, seen through a frightened child's eyes' reading.
- New evidence: fnaf4-plush-two-sprites, fnaf4-plush-eyes-by-scene (implied, because it rests on crops of a fan-compiled map), fnaf4-plush-night6-pupils-only.
- Could not verify frame by frame (YouTube blocked video capture); this is recorded in the evidence notes.
